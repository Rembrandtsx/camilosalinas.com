Eskombros Startup

Eskombros: Transforming Construction Waste Management

Eskombros is a sustainable and technologically advanced solution designed to address one of the most significant challenges in the construction industry: the management of construction and demolition waste (RCD). By offering an end-to-end service, Eskombros helps companies optimize their debris processes, reduce costs, and promote environmental sustainability.

Our Mission

Eskombros aims to revolutionize the construction waste industry by providing an integrated platform that simplifies the collection, transportation, and recycling of debris. Designed for small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) in Latin America, the solution bridges efficiency and sustainability in a traditionally inefficient market.

Who Is It For?

  • SMEs in Construction: Small and medium-sized construction companies seeking efficient waste management solutions.
  • Construction Professionals: Directors, project managers, and logistics coordinators looking to streamline RCD handling.
  • Sustainability Advocates: Companies aiming to comply with environmental regulations and reduce their ecological footprint.

How Eskombros Works

Eskombros provides an all-in-one service to simplify every stage of construction waste management:

  • Collection: On-site pickup of construction and demolition waste.
  • Transportation: Optimized routes to ensure timely delivery to processing centers.
  • Processing: Sorting and recycling of reusable materials.
  • Certifications: Issuance of certificates that ensure compliance with environmental laws and standards.

By managing the entire lifecycle of waste, Eskombros reduces the complexities of coordinating multiple vendors, saves time, and ensures operational transparency.

The Technology Behind Eskombros

Eskombros leverages advanced technologies to optimize operations and provide value to clients:

  • Real-Time Tracking: Monitor waste collection and transportation in real-time to ensure efficiency.
  • Data Analytics: Generate insights for better decision-making and operational improvements.
  • AI Optimization: Use artificial intelligence to enhance route planning, reduce costs, and minimize environmental impact.

Why Choose Eskombros?

Construction companies face growing challenges with waste management, ranging from high costs to regulatory compliance. Eskombros addresses these issues through the following key benefits:

  • Cost Savings: Clients save up to $10 per cubic meter of processed debris compared to traditional services.
  • Operational Efficiency: Streamlined processes reduce delays and improve project timelines.
  • Sustainability: Supports a circular economy by recycling materials and minimizing landfill use.
  • Regulatory Compliance: Helps companies meet local environmental laws and achieve potential tax incentives.
  • Reduced Responsibilities: Eskombros handles all logistics, allowing companies to focus on their core tasks.

Starting Point: Bogotá

Eskombros is launching its services in Bogotá, Colombia, focusing on medium-sized construction firms managing projects with waste volumes between 1,000 and 5,000 cubic meters. This strategic entry point allows Eskombros to work closely with companies open to innovation and technology-driven solutions.

Looking Ahead

Eskombros is not just about managing construction waste; it’s about transforming the industry through efficiency, transparency, and sustainability. By providing a unified, technology-backed solution, Eskombros helps companies reduce their environmental impact, save costs, and focus on what truly matters: building a better future.

Key Takeaways

  • Eskombros provides an all-in-one solution for construction waste management.
  • It reduces costs, enhances operational efficiency, and supports environmental sustainability.
  • Technology like real-time tracking and AI optimization sets it apart from traditional services.
  • Launching in Bogotá, it targets medium-sized construction companies ready for innovation.

Ready to revolutionize your construction waste management? Eskombros is your trusted partner for sustainable and efficient solutions.

TestBuddy Project

TestBuddy is an innovative Visual Studio Code extension designed to automate the process of unit testing in software development. By leveraging natural language processing with GPT-3.5-turbo-16k, TestBuddy generates, regenerates, and modifies unit tests based on user inputs, improving testing efficiency and reducing developer frustration. The tool seamlessly integrates into existing workflows, providing intelligent suggestions to address errors while enhancing code quality. It simplifies unit test creation, allowing developers to focus more on coding rather than repetitive testing tasks.

  • Purpose: Automates the creation, regeneration, and modification of unit tests.
  • Integration: Designed as an extension for Visual Studio Code, ensuring seamless integration into developers' workflows.
  • Technology: Utilizes GPT-3.5-turbo-16k to generate unit tests using natural language processing.
  • User Input: Allows developers to modify or regenerate specific tests based on comments or errors.
  • Back-End: Built with Node.js following the MVC design pattern for scalability.
  • Endpoints: Provides various API routes like generating unit tests, regenerating single tests, and offering feedback on failed tests.
  • Database: Uses Azure SQL Database to store data and support future analytics on user behavior.
  • Deployment: Hosted on Azure App Service with continuous integration using GitHub Actions.
  • Beta Testing: Tested with experienced developers to validate usability, effectiveness, and feedback.
  • Focus: Improves developer efficiency by automating tedious testing tasks while ensuring early detection of errors.
  • Scalability: Designed for long-term growth with a modular and well-structured back-end architecture.
  • UI: Built with JavaScript, offering an intuitive and user-friendly interface.
  • Cost Savings: Reduces the time and resources spent on manual unit test creation.
  • Error Feedback: Provides intelligent suggestions when tests fail, assisting developers with debugging.

Eco2wallet, was funded in 2020 by Yodaly S-R, in the middle of a hard lockdown in London, UK with the main objective of creating a simple way for individuals and retail to be environmentally conscious while redeem their environmental impact with real trees in the Colombian Amazon, because helping the environment via a digital wallet is our motto, and creating an individual market of emissions backed by additionality is our goal

Residia Startup

Residential complexes are an uninformed and disjoint environment which creates arguments between community members, this gets aggrieved by the fact that processes are done in an unorganized way.

  • Arguments between community members (administrators, neighbors, janitors, security guards, etc).
  • Obsolete, neglected and uncommunicative billboards.
  • Inopportune delivery of payment receipts.
  • There is no simple, comfortable and formal way to reserve shared spaces or common areas.
  • Lack of an organized communication method between the community. Insecurity in cases of theft or others.
  • Low communication and poor quality coexistence between community members

Residia creates a digital ecosystem in the residential complexes with the help of different functionalities and technologies, this allows the interaction and integration between the different community members. This promotes process optimization and formalization while improving coexistence which results in a collaborative and transparent community.

Residia is a mobile first platform that creates, develops, and manages residential communities.
